Groups let you organize communities inside OceanX. They appear in the social widget
as the **Lobby** tab.

<Note>
  **Lobby is not live yet.** Release date TBC. [Chat and voice](/social/overview)
  **are** live in the meantime — chat rooms are scoped to the page you're on.
</Note>

## Group types

Groups may be used for:

- Trading teams
- Research groups
- Alpha communities
- Friends
- DAO communities
- Competition teams

## Direct messages

Direct messages are planned.

## Invite and open access

Groups may support both open and invite-based access depending on configuration.

## FAQ

### Are DMs planned?

Yes.

### Can groups be invite only?

Yes, invite-based group access is planned.
