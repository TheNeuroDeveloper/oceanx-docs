OceanX has a social layer built into the trading platform, opened from the social
widget. **Chat and voice are live.** Lobby and the social AI tab are not yet
available.

| Feature | Status |
| --- | --- |
| [Chat](#chat-rooms) | **Live** |
| [Voice](/social/voice) | **Live** — a call inside a chat room |
| [Profiles](/social/profiles) | **Live** |
| [Lobby / groups](/social/groups) | Not live yet |
| Social AI tab | Not live yet |

## Chat rooms

Rooms are **scoped to where you are**, so the conversation matches the screen. A
room exists for Scout, for xStocks (and each ticker you open), for Perpetuals, and
for Predict markets and events.

Open the widget, pick the room, and talk. [Voice](/social/voice) is a call you start
inside whichever room you're in.

## Usernames

Usernames are unique within OceanX and are not anonymous by default. See
[Profiles](/social/profiles).

## Social connections

You can connect **X**, **Discord** and **Telegram** to your account. Telegram
additionally powers [trade alerts through the bot](/integrations/telegram-bot).

ENS is not planned.

## FAQ

### Is chat global or per-page?

Per-page. The room follows the surface you're on rather than being one global feed.

### Are usernames unique?

Yes, unique within OceanX.

### Can I DM someone?

Not yet. Direct messages arrive with [Lobby](/social/groups).
