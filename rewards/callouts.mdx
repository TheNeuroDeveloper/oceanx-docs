Callouts let you publicly call a token on OceanX — and get paid for being right.

Post a callout from any token page, share it, and every trade other users make
on that token through your callout earns you a share of the platform fee, plus
points immediately.

## How it works

1. **Post** — open a token page and hit the megaphone in the header. Write a
   short thesis (140 characters) and post. Your callout appears on the token
   page, in the token's chat room, in the global callouts ticker, and gets its
   own share link (`oceanx.trade/c/…`).
2. **Share** — copy the link or share straight to X. Followers with a linked
   Telegram get a bot notification.
3. **Earn** — when someone opens your callout and trades that token within the
   attribution window, the volume is attributed to you. You earn a share of the
   platform fee on every attributed trade, plus points right away.

## Attribution rules

- Only an **explicit click** on your callout binds a viewer — impressions never
  count.
- Attribution is **last-touch per token** with a 24-hour window: the most
  recent callout a trader clicked for that token gets the credit.
- Your **own trades never count**, and blocked users don't attribute either way.

## Earnings

- **Fee share** — a percentage of the platform fee on each attributed trade,
  accrued in USD and claimable in SOL from the [Rewards](/rewards/points) page
  alongside cashback and referral earnings. Fee share and referral commissions
  stack, but total payouts never exceed the fee collected.
- **Points** — awarded instantly on attributed volume (independent of the
  trader's tier), with daily caps per callout and per creator.

## Who can post

Posting callouts requires a **linked social account** (X, Telegram or Discord —
Settings → Connected accounts) and some **trading history** on OceanX.

Daily limits scale with your [tier](/rewards/points): from 5 callouts per day
at Ripple up to 1,000 per day at Poseidon. One live callout per token at a
time, with a short cooldown between callouts on the same token.

## Leaderboard

The **Top callers** leaderboard on the Rewards page ranks creators by the
volume their callouts drove. Developers can read callouts through the
[public API](/api/endpoints/callouts).
