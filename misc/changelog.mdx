## Recent releases

### 2026-08 — Spot quotes & exit check

[Exit Check](https://www.oceanx.trade/exit-check) shipped: quote a ladder of
position sizes to see whether a whole bag can actually be sold, and at what price.
The same engine is exposed through the Developer API — see
[Spot Quotes](/api/spot).

### 2026-08 — xStocks

[xStocks](/markets/xstocks) opened to production, subject to
[regional restrictions](/getting-started/supported-regions).

### 2026-08 — Social chat & voice

[Chat](/social/overview) rooms went live, scoped to the page you're on, with
[voice calls](/social/voice) inside any room. Lobby and direct messages are still to
come.
