xStocks are tokenized equities, giving on-chain exposure to stocks and ETFs through
partner infrastructure. **Live** on OceanX at
[oceanx.trade/stocks](https://www.oceanx.trade/stocks).

You get a discovery grid of the available tickers with live prices and charts, and a
per-ticker page to trade from. Swaps settle on Solana like any other token.

<Warning>
  **xStocks are region-restricted.** Access is geofenced, and OceanX will redirect
  you if your region is not eligible. See
  [Supported regions](/getting-started/supported-regions).
</Warning>

## What an xStock is — and is not

An xStock gives economic exposure to the underlying stock or ETF through a tokenized
certificate. It is **not** an ordinary share and carries **no voting rights**.

Solana xStocks keep raw token balances constant across corporate actions; dividends
and splits are applied through an official multiplier, which OceanX applies when
displaying your balance.

## Restrictions

Beyond the regional geofence, some features may require identity verification or
partner-level compliance checks.

Requirements will depend on the underlying provider.
