OceanX Perpetuals provide leveraged trading inside OceanX, aggregated across
multiple venues — **Hyperliquid**, **Lighter**, **Aster** and **Pacifica** — so you
can compare a market before choosing where to trade it.

<Note>
Perpetuals are not live yet. Release date TBC.
</Note>

## Trading from OceanX

Users can trade directly from their OceanX wallet within OceanX.

No separate interface is required.

## Order types

Perpetuals support market and limit order entry.

Spot trading has its own order types — see [Orders](/trading/orders).

## Features

Perpetuals include:

- Market and limit order entry
- Market selection
- Leverage controls
- Order book
- Charting
- Position management
- Live PnL
- Position history

## Fees

Each venue charges its own fees. Applicable fees are shown in the OceanX interface
before you confirm.

## Risk

Perpetual futures involve leverage and can result in significant losses, including liquidation of your position.

Understand liquidation risk before trading.
